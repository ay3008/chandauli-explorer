Nature · People · Tradition

Where nature and heritage meet.

Chandauli is known as the “Dhaan ka Katora” of Uttar Pradesh, reflecting the importance of rice cultivation in the district. Its landscape includes the Chakia plateau, the plains and the Ganga region.

  • ✦Explore forests and natural landscapes.
  • ✦Discover local heritage and spiritual sites.
  • ✦Learn about farming and rural livelihoods.

Explore responsibly

Make every journey count.

Plan your visit, support local communities and help preserve the natural and cultural places you discover.

01

Plan before you go

Check road conditions, access rules, weather and local guidance before setting out.

02

Respect local life

Ask permission before photographing people, respect religious customs and leave private property undisturbed.

03

Keep it beautiful

Carry litter back, protect wildlife and stay away from unsafe water edges and restricted areas.
